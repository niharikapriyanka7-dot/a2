Care and repair for classic clothes

The habits and repairs that keep coats, shoes, knitwear and denim looking good for decades.

Daily habits

A little care, every day

  • Hang coats and jackets on wide, shaped hangers as soon as you take them off.
  • Brush wool with a clothes brush to lift dust and refresh the nap.
  • Rest shoes for a day between wears and use cedar shoe trees.
  • Air knitwear before folding it away.
  • Empty pockets so garments keep their shape.
Repairs worth making

What to fix and when

Seasonal storage

Putting clothes away for the season

  • Clean before storing: moths are attracted to food and skin residue.
  • Use breathable covers, not plastic, for coats and suits.
  • Fold knitwear with acid-free tissue in drawers or boxes.
  • Add cedar or lavender to help deter moths.
  • Check stored clothes every couple of months.
