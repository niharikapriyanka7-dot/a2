About Classic Apparel

An independent guide to timeless clothing for people who’d rather buy once and wear for years.

Our story

Why classic clothes?

Classic Apparel grew out of a love of clothes with a story: the coat that’s been worn for twenty winters, the shoes that have been resoled three times, the denim jacket that only gets better. These pieces prove that good clothing doesn’t need to change every season.

We write about the enduring garments of everyday dressing, their history, how to recognise quality and how to wear them today, along with practical care and repair advice. Our free tools help you explore classics, borrow style from past decades and keep your clothes in good condition.

Classic Apparel is independent. We don’t sell clothing and aren’t paid to recommend brands.
